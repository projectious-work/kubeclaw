# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In summary: be respectful, be constructive, and help make this a welcoming
space for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

KubeClaw is a learning project. Questions that seem basic are welcome, and
"I think this is wrong because…" is a contribution, not an attack. Please
extend the same assumption of good faith to others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption are not tolerated. The full
definitions are in the Contributor Covenant text linked above.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project
maintainer at **bnaard@gmx.net**. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that
violate this code, and may block contributors who persist.
